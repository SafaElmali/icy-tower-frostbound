# Climb menu

Mode: Operate

THESIS: Choose a mode quickly and understand what changes before starting.

OWN-WORLD: Extend the homepage's frozen arcade identity, condensed italic display type, pale ice primary control, dark navy surfaces, and restrained warm accents.

STORY: Select Classic, Party, or Practice; inspect its mechanics; start the climb. Additional activities remain grouped below. Progress and settings share the shell.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact title and tabs, stacked native radio choices beside a mode-specific geometric jump diagram and explanation, then secondary activities. A fixed footer keeps the current play action visible. Phones stack the content and scroll within the dialog.

FORM: A split selector, considered alongside a compact list and a carousel. The split version best supports direct comparison without hiding choices. User delegated direction selection; this is an extension of the established homepage system, with no new visual-world workshop or comp.

Verification: 1440×900, 762×843, 390×844; pointer and arrow-key mode selection; progress/settings tabs; typecheck, lint, static Netlify build. Existing game engine behavior, modal focus management, disabled-mode rules, and callbacks retained.
