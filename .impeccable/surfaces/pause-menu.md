# Pause menu

Mode: Operate

THESIS: Resume a paused climb immediately, with the current floor and score easy to scan.

OWN-WORLD: Extend the frozen arcade identity using Frostbound Display, a deep navy panel, ice primary action and peach keyboard focus.

STORY: Confirm the run is paused, glance at floor and points, resume; optionally access settings or the gameplay guide, or return to title with the existing leave confirmation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A centered 440px panel replaces the oversized editorial block. Paused is the main heading, followed by supporting copy, two equal stats, the full-width Resume action, paired Settings / How to play controls, and a quiet title action. Close is attached to the panel and resumes the run.

FORM: Selected over a side drawer and split stats/navigation layout. Scoped extension of the established world; no new comp or raster. Native dialog focus, Escape/P/Enter resume handling, existing callbacks and leave confirmation retained. At 390px the whole menu fits without scrolling, with 44–56px actions. Gameplay logic and game-over visual composition remain unchanged.
