# Character menu

Mode: Operate

THESIS: Make choosing an earned outfit immediate while keeping the live climber visible.

OWN-WORLD: Extend the frozen arcade identity of the redesigned menus: condensed italic display, navy backdrop, ice completion action, peach equipped state, actual rendered game character.

STORY: See your climber, choose a category, equip a color, understand locked rewards, then return to the game.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop has a larger live preview at left and three category tabs with compact choice rows at right. Phones put a short preview above the category selector. A persistent Done footer explains automatic browser saving.

FORM: Chosen autonomously over a full catalog and a step-by-step picker. Keep real cosmetics, progress, unlock rules, automatic equip/save, preview playback and storage-unavailable copy. Native radios convey exclusive choice and disabled locks; Base UI tabs/dialog preserve keyboard behavior. No generated artwork or new-world comp is needed for this extension. Shared multiplayer preview styling remains unchanged through scoped wardrobe CSS.
